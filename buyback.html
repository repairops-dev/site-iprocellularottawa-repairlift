Before you sell

Ownership

You must be the owner of the device, or have the owner's permission to sell it. We do not buy lost or stolen devices.

Accounts and locks

Sign out of iCloud or your Google account and turn off Find My or activation lock before the sale. Locked devices may not be accepted.

Your data

Back up and erase your data before you sell. We remind you to wipe the device before the sale is final, and we are not responsible for data left on a device after a sale.

When you sell us a device and accept payment, the sale is final. Full details in our Warranty / Refund Policy.
