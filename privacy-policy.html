Privacy policy

Last updated: October 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use and protect personal information when you visit this website, contact us, or buy, sell or service a device with us.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly, such as your name, phone number, email address and device details, when you contact us, request a quote or use our services. We also collect basic website data, such as browser type, pages visited, approximate location and cookie data, and call information, such as the phone number you call from and the date, time and length of the call, when calls are logged or measured by call tracking tools. Payment information is handled by our payment processors, and we do not keep full card numbers.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to respond to inquiries, provide and improve our services, schedule and complete work, process payments, keep records required by law, measure and improve our website and advertising, and prevent fraud. We do not sell your personal information.

3. Communications

By providing your contact details, you consent to be contacted by us regarding your inquiry or service. You may opt out of marketing messages at any time; consent to marketing is not a condition of purchase.

4. Sharing and Service Providers

We are the business responsible for your information. We share information only as needed to provide our services or as required by law. This includes payment processors and RepairOps.AI, our website hosting and marketing service provider, which receives the requests you send through our website forms and delivers them to our team through its lead management and customer relationship management (CRM) tools, which may include GoHighLevel. RepairOps.AI handles your information only to provide these services to us. We may also use call tracking, email and text messaging providers. We do not share your information with third parties for their own marketing.

5. Cookies and Advertising

This website may use cookies and similar tools, including tools from advertising platforms such as Google, to understand how visitors use the site and to measure our ads and the calls and form submissions that result from them. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Some site features may not work as expected if cookies are disabled.

6. Data on Your Device

When you bring us a device, we do not intentionally access your personal files. You are responsible for backing up your data before service. We are not responsible for data loss.

7. Data Retention & Security

We keep your information only as long as needed for the purposes described here, or as long as the law requires, and take reasonable administrative, technical and physical measures to protect it. No method of storage or transfer is completely secure.

8. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information by contacting us using the details below, subject to applicable law. We may need to confirm your identity first.

9. Children

This website is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.
